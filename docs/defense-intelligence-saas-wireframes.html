Defense Intelligence SaaS

Proposed convergence architecture and low-fidelity product wireframes. The layouts combine the Defense Intelligence SaaS foundation, Sabre's scan-first research model, and Opportunity Intelligence's guided capture lifecycle.

1. Product convergence architecture

One platform consumes the strongest capability from each current product while maintaining separate evidence, management, response, and automation planes.

2. Command Center wireframe

The landing page is a daily decision and work surface. It avoids a generic dashboard of charts.

3. Opportunity Queue wireframe

Sabre's compact table becomes the canonical pipeline, with owner, phase, due urgency, next action, evidence, and readiness added to the default scan path.

4. Guided Opportunity Detail wireframe

The detail route combines Opportunity Intelligence's lifecycle shell, Sabre's source-backed research, and Defense Intelligence's budget, award, and transaction lineage.

Low-fidelity concept only. The design document defines the normative product, data, security, and migration requirements.
